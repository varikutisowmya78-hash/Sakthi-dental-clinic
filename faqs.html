FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some common questions about dental care and treatments at Sakthi Dental Clinic.

1. How often should I visit the dentist?

It is generally recommended to visit the dentist regularly for check-ups and professional cleaning. Your dentist can suggest a suitable schedule based on your oral health.

2. Is dental cleaning painful?

Dental cleaning is usually comfortable. Some people may experience mild sensitivity depending on the condition of their teeth and gums.

3. What should I do if I have a toothache?

If you have a toothache, it is best to consult a dentist to identify the cause. Avoid delaying treatment if the pain continues or becomes severe.

4. Can cavities be prevented?

Regular brushing, flossing, limiting sugary foods and drinks, and regular dental check-ups can help reduce the risk of cavities.

5. How can I keep my teeth healthy?

Brush your teeth twice a day, clean between your teeth, maintain a balanced diet and visit your dentist regularly for check-ups.

6. Are dental treatments safe?

Dental treatments are planned according to the patient's individual condition. Your dentist will explain the recommended treatment and answer your questions before proceeding.

7. What treatments are available at the clinic?

The clinic provides various dental care services such as dental check-ups, teeth cleaning, fillings, gum care, root canal treatment and other dental procedures.

8. How can I book an appointment?

You can contact Sakthi Dental Clinic through the Contact page to enquire about an appointment and available timings.
